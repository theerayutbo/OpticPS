## Uncertainty

Statistical uncertainty is the sampling variation of the error counts: Clopper-Pearson 95% intervals over the pooled symbols, which assume independent errors, and a bootstrap over frames. Frames repeat one payload, so these intervals are optimistic and describe noise only. Physical measurement uncertainty (instrument calibration, power meter, attenuator and bandwidth accuracy) is a separate matter, is not part of any interval above, and is not recorded unless the sidecar's calibration field gives it. For these captures it is stated per capture in the uncertainty tables.

## Limitations

- No real UXR export has been imported by this project; the importer follows the vendor guide and was tested on synthetic files. Treat every capture here accordingly.
- Sample loss of less than about 1 symbol, synchronous with the frame period on one waveform in every run, may not be detected.
- Lower error rate or lower symbol energy is not a throughput gain.
