# Changelog

## 1.2.0 (2026-10-30)
Scope export import (HDF5, CSV, BIN, layouts from the UXR Programmer's Guide), a linear non-periodic capture analysis with preamble-based frame finding, symbol-clock estimation and decision-directed timing tracking across a frame, lost-frame accounting, `opticps ingest-uxr` and `opticps compare-uxr` (paired comparison, fairness audit, report), a content-hash leakage registry, `optical_received_power_dBm` may be null (not measured), and the Southampton UXR protocol. `analyze` refuses measured captures. Tested on synthetic files only; no real UXR export has been seen. Transmitter waveforms are unchanged.

Rework 3 of the UXR work: `compare-uxr` separates set-points (symbol rate, AWG and capture sample rates, electrical amplitude, fiber length, attenuation, receiver bandwidth), which must match, from measured outcomes (launch power, OMA, extinction ratio), which are reported and never gate; received power is reported with its spread and gates only beyond `--power-tolerance-db` (default 0.5 dB) with a warning. A set-point not recorded in all or some captures gives a verdict with a "not recorded: X" caveat. Comparisons are made per condition (profile, attenuation, fiber length), so a sweep gives one verdict per point. With 3 or more regularly spaced preambles the frame-spacing clock stands and a large in-frame tracker residual is a note, not a failure. The reference-mismatch check now uses the known training block (payload error far above what training predicts) instead of an SNR and SER gate; flagged captures stay in the comparison, counted and named. Clean errors for `inf`/`nan` unit codes and non-object sidecars, bounded HDF5 metadata, results without a sidecar counted in a warning, and a `/Frame` dataset gives model unknown with a note. Waveform CSV files are unchanged.

Rework 4 of the UXR work: a pair's verdict is withheld when any capture it uses is flagged `reference_mismatch_suspected` (flagged captures stay counted and listed); with a firm clock, a tracker-versus-spacing disagreement now clears `rate_estimate_reliable` when the payload or training symbols disagree too (equal dropped samples at every frame gap); HDF5 metadata reads take only the first element, bound all text, cap attributes at 256 and still accept variable-length strings; conditions round attenuation to 0.1 dB and length to 0.001 km; `--power-tolerance-db` rejects negative or non-finite values; a string-valued numeric optional field is a clean validation error; `compare-uxr` prints withheld results as lines.

Receiver-analysis report: `opticps uxr-report --results DIR [--output-dir reports] [--simulation results/synthetic/demo_results.json]` builds a PDF and Markdown report from `ingest-uxr` and `compare-uxr` outputs (new `reporting/uxr_report.py`, reusing `reporting/report.py`): per waveform, transmitted and received excerpts, recovered symbol distribution against the target, eye diagram, SER and BER with intervals, SNR, level means and spreads, R_SMD and R_BMD with `rate_estimate_reliable`, receiver acquisition settings, comparison with the nearest simulated point, statistical intervals kept apart from physical measurement uncertainty (not recorded unless the sidecar gives it), verdicts, withheld reasons, flagged captures and the set-point audit. The header states the data source; any simulated capture prints `MOCK/SIMULATED CAPTURES: NOT LABORATORY MEASUREMENTS`, and every figure carries its data source. `ingest-uxr` now also saves `*_excerpt.npz` (received samples of symbols 480 to 543 of the first frame) and per-level means and spreads. `reports/UXR_Receiver_Analysis_Example` is built from mock-receiver captures (`scripts/make_uxr_example.py`). A pair short of paired runs because unreliable captures flagged `reference_mismatch_suspected` were excluded now names the flag; flagged captures stay counted and listed with their pair's verdict withheld (docs and warning text corrected); an HDF5 `/Frame/TheFrame` holding a variable-length string is a clean import error. Waveform CSV files are unchanged.

Matched filter in the capture chain: `analyze_scope_capture` (and `ingest-uxr`) now integrate the record over one symbol period, the rectangular NRZ matched filter that `process_capture` already applied to periodic records, by default (`matched_filter=True`, `--no-matched-filter` to turn it off; recorded as `matched_filter` in the analysis and the report). Sampling at symbol centres without it cost about 1.9 dB SNR (SER 71 against 4 errors at 18 dB in the review); the capture chain now agrees with `process_capture` on the same record within 0.3 dB SNR and 15 percent in error count (tested), and the mock example's SER at 18.4 dB fell from 1.5e-3 to about 9e-5. Timing, clock and sampling phase are still fitted on the unfiltered record. The exported waveforms are NRZ; an RRC waveform needs a different filter, so a manifest that says RRC is refused unless the filter is turned off. `gain_v_per_level` is now that of the filtered record (a few percent below the pulse amplitude). The report's simulation comparison no longer says the simulation is "generic"; it states the real differences (capture sample rate, receiver chain, noise realisation, power offset), a zero-error row shows only a one-sided upper bound, and a figure heading stays with its figure in the PDF. The eye data of the example (`results/uxr_example/*_eye.npz`) is git-ignored and regenerated by `scripts/make_uxr_example.py`; `uxr-report` on a fresh clone omits eye figures until then. Waveform CSV files are unchanged.

Known limitations in 1.2.0: a sample loss of fewer than about 1 symbol, synchronous with the frame period on one waveform in every run, may not be detected (physically implausible, not impossible). No real UXR export has been imported; every receiver-side item has run on synthetic and mock-receiver files only.

## 1.1.0 (2026-10-30)
Selectable fiber model based on Rec. ITU-T G.652 (08/2024): `none`, G.652.D representative, dispersion-bound presets and custom, with value provenance. `LinkConfig` gains `dispersion_slope_ps_per_nm2_km` (third-order term, beta3) and `fiber`; without them the link is numerically identical to 1.0.0. The four fiber fields default to unset, an unset slope is the 1.0.0 second-order model, and a field that conflicts with `fiber` raises. Each fiber value, including the slope, has its own provenance tag. `cd_null_frequency_hz` returns infinity for zero dispersion or length. Optional `fiber` metadata in the capture schema. New `opticps fiber-validate`, `configs/fiber_validation.yaml`, `results/fiber_validation/`, `reports/Fiber_Model_Validation_Report`, and a fiber section and slide in the report and deck. Version metadata in waveform packages was not regenerated; waveform CSV files are unchanged. No measured data; nothing experimentally validated.

Known low-severity issues in 1.1.0: capture `fiber` length accepts NaN; wavelength has no provenance tag; the no-fiber `LinkConfig` path does not range-check raw values; mock captures omit fiber metadata; a fiber preset with no length silently means 0 km.

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-30)
First release. M8199B waveform packages for two profiles, exact CCDM, IM/DD link simulation, receiver DSP, capture ingest, fair comparison tools, simulated demonstration, report and slides. No measured data. M8199B playback not verified.
