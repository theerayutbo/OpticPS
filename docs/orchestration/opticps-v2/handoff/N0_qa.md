## MAJOR
1. Plan: B2B cannot fix what the SSMF holdout tests, and the plan does not say so. Chirp has no effect in B2B, yet it moves the SSMF null. Repro: f = sqrt(c/(2 lambda^2 D L)) with D 15.95 gives 29.4 GHz at 4.53 km and 28 GHz at 5 km; workbook Fig.1 fading has its SMF minimum at 28 GHz; the paper says ~25 GHz (p.2, 5 km) and "around 24 GHz" measured (p.6). The ~5 GHz gap exceeds the C6 tolerance of 2 GHz. Chirp is not in rule 6, and a fit cannot reach it, so it becomes an assumption chosen after the prose was read. D of the actual spools is also unmeasured. Also: no identifiability check on the (up to 6) Tx/Rx parameters. Also: the paper calls NANF penalty-free relative to B2B (p.4), so copying the B2B curve would meet C1 and C2 with no fibre model; shared apparatus is handled by the label, not at criterion level. Required: name chirp and freeze its value and source before fitting; add an identifiability check; report a B2B-copy baseline for C1 and C2.
2. Plan: primary criteria and fit rules are not fully defined. (a) C1: the Fig.4 axis starts at -12 dBm and the paper puts the 80 Gb/s crossing at "around -12 dBm", so it may be unbracketed; only interpolation is defined, and the decision rule needs every rate. The cell was deliberately not read. (b) C3: "penalty" has no ROP, statistic or interpolation rule. (c) The -5 dBm exclusion and censoring are written for scoring only; the calibration row keeps B2B -4, -3, -2 dBm (which the plan calls spurious) and does not say how BER=0 enters the fit. Censoring and the exclusion are otherwise sound for scoring. Required: dated amendment and new pin.

But Sources section 5 and the handoff redirect C6 to Fig.8 in an unpinned document, which the plan's amendment rule forbids. Required: dated amendment and new pin before N1 scoring, naming the sheet and a null estimator that excludes the AWG clock-leakage spikes in Fig.8 (p.6; not mentioned in the docs).

The paper says subcarriers at very low SNR around the SSMF null were not data-loaded (p.7), so these are most likely unloaded subcarriers (inference), not "unusable" or "missing" cells. The doc never reports the formula, so "cannot be scored on Fig.11" is unsupported; the band itself marks the null. Plan handling must be decided, not assumed.
