## PAM-M rates (`optimization/rates.py`, v1.3-mpam C2)

Derived independently from the definitions; no other simulator was consulted. On the unipolar alphabet x_i = i, i = 0..M-1, with noise sigma:

- PSNR = (M-1)^2 / sigma^2, so sigma = (M-1) / 10^(PSNR_dB/20). M = 4 gives 9 / sigma^2 as before. Equal PSNR across M is equal peak-to-peak swing and equal noise. The average SNR E[x^2] / sigma^2 is reported separately (`average_snr`).
- R_SMD = I(X;Y) = H(X) - H(X|Y).
- R_BMD = [H(X) - sum_{k=1..m} H(B_k|Y)]^+, m = log2 M, Gray labels, exact bit posteriors; Boecherer et al. 2015 (the AIR row above). Its unclipped value is returned as `bmd_raw` and can be negative; `bmd` is the explicit clip. Without the clip, H(X) - sum_k H(B_k|Y) is the GMI at s = 1 of the metric q(x,y) = prod_k P(b_k|y) / P_X(x) (the denominator sum_x' P_X(x') q(x',y) is then 1). It is not the GMI at s = 1 of q = prod_k P(b_k|y): for M = 8, exp(-0.1 x^2), 20 dB the two are 1.43647 and 1.40726 (tested). The GMI also maximises over s >= 0, which is not done, so the code keeps the name R_BMD.
- Expectations over Gaussian noise, equal sigma on all levels: Gauss-Hermite quadrature (log-sum-exp posteriors). Default 120 nodes for M = 4 (unchanged, bit-identical), 240 otherwise. Measured over PSNR 8-30 dB in 2 dB steps with three PMFs per order (uniform, exp(-0.05 x^2), a seeded Dirichlet): 120 nodes are 1.9e-8 (M = 16), 2.6e-8 (M = 2), 3.7e-8 (M = 8) and 4.1e-8 (M = 4) from the 320-node value; 240 nodes are within 1.5e-10 (M = 16), 7.6e-11 (M = 2) and 4.2e-10 (M = 8) of 320 nodes (5.8e-10 at M = 4, not the default), and within 3.9e-10 of an independent adaptive Gauss-Kronrod integral (`scipy.integrate.quad_vec`, own posteriors, hand-written Gray tables) and of an independent 2^15-point trapezoid at M = 2, 8, 16. Correction: the "6.7e-10 vs an independent trapezoid at 8-30 dB" given earlier was a Gauss-Legendre reference at M = 8, 34 dB, not an independent trapezoid over 8-30 dB; the figure measured now is 3.9e-10 (worst M = 8). Comparisons finer than 1e-7 across orders must fix `nodes` on both sides.
- Unequal per-level sigma: the per-symbol Gauss-Hermite rule is not reliable. Nodes for level i are centred on mu_i with scale sigma_i, so the narrower Gaussians of other levels are not resolved. Worst error in bit (max of SMD and bmd_raw) of the legacy path against the common-grid integrator, PSNR 8-30 dB in 2 dB steps (12 values), six sigma patterns (four seeded log-uniform draws with the extreme factors forced onto two levels, one ascending and one descending geometric ramp), sigma factors in [lo, hi], spread = hi / lo:

  | spread (lo-hi) | M=2 120 / 240 | M=4 120 / 240 | M=8 120 / 240 | M=16 120 / 240 |

  | 2 (0.7-1.4) | 1.7e-6 / 5.3e-8 | 1.0e-6 / 3.7e-8 | 3.6e-7 / 1.2e-8 | 1.8e-7 / 4.9e-9 |
  | 4 (0.5-2) | 1.3e-4 / 9.8e-6 | 2.0e-4 / 1.7e-5 | 5.1e-5 / 1.4e-6 | 5.6e-5 / 3.3e-6 |
  | 9 (0.33-3) | 1.0e-2 / 1.5e-3 | 6.3e-3 / 1.5e-3 | 5.3e-3 / 1.1e-3 | 2.6e-3 / 6.1e-4 |
  | 16 (0.25-4) | 4.0e-2 / 1.1e-2 | 6.6e-2 / 1.6e-2 | 5.1e-2 / 7.2e-3 | 1.7e-2 / 5.3e-3 |
  | 64 (0.125-8) | 0.20 / 0.17 | 0.23 / 0.13 | 0.15 / 5.6e-2 | 0.15 / 0.10 |

  The common-grid integrator below is the reference for these entries (it agrees with the adaptive reference to 1e-13); the patterns force the stated extremes, so these are worse than a plain uniform draw in [lo, hi]. The earlier ratio-range table (0.7-1.4 at 240 nodes "2e-9") came from a different, milder draw and is superseded.
- Common-y composite Gauss-Legendre integrator (`_conditional_entropies_common_grid`): every quantity is integral p(x,y) f(x,y) dy over one y axis with the joint density p(x,y) = P(x) N(y; mu_x, sd_x^2) in log-sum-exp form. Panel edges are at mu_i + t sd_i for every level with P(i) > 0 and every integer t = -12..12 (so each Gaussian is resolved at its own scale, from its mean outwards, and the range covers mu_i +- 12 sd_i of every level), 10 Gauss-Legendre nodes per panel, edges closer than 1e-6 min(sd) merged. The integrated joint density must equal 1 to 1e-9 or a ValueError is raised. Panel order justified by self-convergence against 32 nodes on half-sd panels (M = 2, 4, 8, 16; 8-30 dB, 2 dB steps; six patterns; spreads 2, 4, 9, 16, 64 and 400 at ranges 0.05-20), worst of SMD and bmd_raw: 6 nodes 7.3e-10, 8 nodes 5.0e-12, 10 nodes 1.0e-13 (chosen), 12 nodes 4.0e-15, 16 nodes 4.0e-15; sd-edge spacing 2 with 16 nodes 1.1e-12; panels at the means only (48 nodes) 3e-3 (M = 2) to 4e-2 (M = 16), and that grid fails the mass check. Against `scipy.integrate.quad_vec` (adaptive Gauss-Kronrod, own posteriors, own Gray tables, break points at mu +- {0, 1, 3, 6} sd) over M = 4, 8, 16, spreads 2, 4, 9, 16, 64, 8-30 dB, six patterns (1080 cases), worst of SMD and bmd_raw: M = 4 1.0e-13, M = 8 1.4e-14, M = 16 4.0e-15; overall 1.04e-13. Equal sigma: within 3.5e-13 of the adaptive reference (M = 2) and 5e-15 (M = 16).
- Routing (`quadrature="auto"`): common grid for M != 4 when max(sd)/min(sd) > 1 + 1e-12, Gauss-Hermite otherwise (equal-sigma numbers unchanged). M = 4 with the default quadrature always uses the legacy kernel, so the PAM4 anchor stays byte-identical (committed path: spread 1.0050 in all 90 adaptive calls, no warning, anchor green with `-W error::UserWarning`). `quadrature="common_grid"` forces the new integrator at any M for new callers. `Rates` records `sigma_spread` and `quadrature`; for the common grid `nodes` is the number of y points. A max/min above 3.0 at M = 4 on the legacy path is unreliable and emits a UserWarning that quotes the measured error. Threshold: for M = 4 at 120 nodes, over PSNR 0-45 dB (3 dB steps plus 8-30 dB in 2 dB steps) and 26 sigma patterns, the worst error is 4.9e-5 at spread 3, 9.2e-5 at 3.3, 1.4e-4 at 3.4 (first above 1e-4), 3.5e-4 at 4; on the 8-30 dB, six pattern grid the crossing is between 3.6 (7.7e-5) and 4 (2.0e-4). The threshold 3.0 is below the first crossing, where the measured error is about half of 1e-4. Cost, one `gaussian_rates` call with the common grid, median of 40 (Intel i5-8259U, Python 3.11, numpy 2.4.6): M = 4 1.6 ms (990 y points), M = 8 3.6 ms (1990), M = 16 10 ms (3990), independent of spread and PSNR; Gauss-Hermite 240 nodes at M = 16 takes 15 ms.

Tests: `tests/test_mpam_rates.py` checks closed-form SER 2(1-1/M)Q(1/(2 sigma)) by Monte Carlo (M = 2, 8, 16; PAM2 at 12 dB = 0.0233; a 6 dB error is detected), binary-AWGN capacity by `scipy.integrate.quad`, PAM2 and PAM4 embeddings in larger alphabets, trapezoid and adaptive Gauss-Kronrod integrators with hand-written Gray tables (equal sigma at M = 8 and 16; unequal sigma, common grid against the adaptive reference at M = 4, 8, 16 for spreads 2, 4, 9, 16, 64, tolerance 1e-11), routing and warning tests, the GMI identity with the denominator summed explicitly for both metrics (1.43647 and 1.40726 pinned), the bounds 0 <= SMD <= H <= log2 M and BMD <= SMD, and exact PAM4 values pinned at de1fc73.

## Symmetric shaping family and symmetric CCDM quantiser (v1.3-mpam C3)

Derived independently from the definitions; no other simulator was consulted.

- Pair family: q_j = P(+-x_j), j = 1..M/2, inner pair first, sum q_j = 1/2, so P is symmetric and E[x] = 0. `symmetric_from_pairs` and `pairs_of` are exact inverses; `pairs_of` rejects asymmetric PMFs.
- Maxwell-Boltzmann sign convention (unchanged from the repo): P(x) proportional to exp(-lam x^2), lam > 0 inner-heavy, lam < 0 outer-heavy (PS70 has lam = +ln(0.35/0.15)/8 > 0). `mb_for_entropy(H, M, sign)` solves H(MB(sign * mu)) = H for mu >= 0 with Brent's method; dH/dlam = -lam Var(x^2), so H falls monotonically in |lam|. Reachable range 1 < H <= log2 M for M >= 4 (lam -> +-inf leaves two levels, 1 bit, never attained); M = 2 only H = 1. Out of range raises ValueError.
- MB(+lam) and MB(-lam) have equal entropy only at M = 4 among non-trivial orders (M = 2 is uniform for every lam; two pair weights, a two-point law swaps under lam -> -lam). Measured at lam = 0.2: M = 8 gives 1.708 versus 1.074 bits, so the entropy match is done per side; tested at M = 8 and 16.
- Symmetric quantiser: quantise the lower half (outermost first, 2q scaled to n/2 symbols) with the existing largest-remainder rule and mirror. n must be even. Total is exactly n and pairs are never split. The legacy `quantize_composition` is untouched and can split a pair on tied remainders (uniform PAM4, n = 6 gives (2, 2, 1, 1); the symmetric rule gives (2, 1, 1, 2)).
- M = 4 equivalence: over all even n in 2..2048 and 101 PMFs (PS70, PS80, Outer70, uniform, 97 p_outer values from 0.01 to 0.49), the two rules differ in 3330 of 103424 cases and every difference is a case where the legacy composition is asymmetric or raises (no difference where the legacy result is symmetric). At the listed n only: 32, 64, 128, 256, 512, 1000, 1024, 2048, 4096, 16384 (tested) and 65536 (checked by hand), which cover the config block length 1024 and the demo sweep, they are identical on every one of the 101 PMFs. Legacy paths were therefore not switched.
- Finite-block loss: R = floor(log2 W)/n with W the type class size; `symmetric_rate_loss` = H(p) - R = [H(p) - H(counts/n)] + [H(counts/n) - log2(W)/n] + [log2(W)/n - R]. Checked against a log-gamma evaluation of W.
- Decode failures: a block with the wrong composition raises `CompositionError`, a symbol outside the alphabet or of non-integer dtype raises `InvalidSymbolError` (a `CompositionError`), a block of the right composition ranked at or above 2^k raises `RankOverflowError`. `try_decode` returns a typed failure for any bad input, including wrong length, scalar, empty, None, ragged (`BlockShapeError`). This detects malformed blocks only: composition-changing errors raise `CompositionError`, but composition-preserving errors are almost always silent, about 99.5% at PS70, n = 256 (4 seeds, 1000 swaps of two unequal symbols each: 672-699 decoded silently to wrong bits, 2-4 were rank overflow, the rest were equal-symbol swaps that change nothing); overflow needs a swap at a very early position. A +1/-1 pair preserves the composition only when it swaps adjacent values. Block erasure in C6 must report this as a limit and rely on an outer code. The symmetric quantiser is closest to n p in L1 over symmetric compositions, not KL-optimal (half p = [0.0256, 0.2324, 0.0532, 0.1888], n = 28: (1,7,1,5) against the KL-better (1,6,2,5)), and raises when a positive p gets a zero count. Profile: `docs/notes/ccdm_profile.md`.

## Generic symmetric optimiser, constraints and acquisition guard (v1.3-mpam C4)

Derived independently from the definitions; no other simulator was consulted.

- Family and bounds: pair probabilities q_j = P(+-x_j), inner pair first, sum q_j = 1/2, box q_j in [1/n, 1/2] with n the block length (1/n is the smallest probability that can quantise to a nonzero count, so an optimum at the floor is representable; the box needs n >= M). Neither PLAN nor the roadmap states a bound: this one is a recorded decision. An earlier floor of 1/(4n) put the optimum at a count of zero, which the matcher rejects, so every start endpoint was infeasible over most of the sweep and selection fell back to a Maxwell-Boltzmann grid. Every optimum reported is over this constrained set, and the selection reports which pairs sit at the floor. Dimension M/2: 2, 4, 8 for M = 4, 8, 16. This is a higher-dimensional family than the one-parameter Maxwell-Boltzmann or p_outer families; MB PMFs are inside it.
- Concavity: I(X;Y) is concave in the input PMF for a fixed channel, and P is a linear function of q, so the continuous SMD objective is concave on the box (a convex subset of the simplex, so concavity holds on it). A local maximum over the box is then global over the box for that objective. The claim made in code is "global for the unquantised SMD objective over the constrained set q_j >= 1/n, on this channel estimate" and nothing wider. It is checked on midpoints (and 3:1 points) of random box points at M = 4, 8, 16 in `tests/test_mpam_adaptive.py`; a multistart agreement check is logged with every selection. R_BMD is not shown concave, so for it the claim is "best of N starts". N = 11: uniform, MB inner and outer at entropy deficits 0.12, 0.28, 0.6 bit below log2 M, four Dirichlet draws from `SeedSequence([seed, 3])`. MB starts outside the box are mapped in (`lower + max(q - lower, 0) * scale`, identity inside the box) and flagged `mapped_into_bounds`.
- Objective and staircase: rate(P) - rate_loss(quantize_symmetric_composition(P, n)), with rate_loss = H(counts / n) - floor(log2 W) / n (the legacy objective's form). The loss depends on P only through integer counts, so it is piecewise constant; the continuous rate is fitted (SLSQP on x = 2q, finite-difference gradient step 1e-6, ftol 1e-12; BMD fitted as the smooth bmd_raw and reported clipped), then the exact quantised objective is evaluated at every start endpoint, at uniform and on an MB grid of both signs (10 entropy deficits from 0.04 to 0.95 bit). The argmax over the in-bounds, feasible logged candidates is selected. It is the best candidate tried, not a proven staircase optimum. The uniform PMF is the direct mapping at M a power of two (bits straight onto labels, as `frame.py` `direct_uniform`): it pays no matcher loss, and the candidates, `evaluate_menu`, `fixed_menu`, `best_fixed` and every reported gain charge it 0. The legacy `select_distribution` still charges its uniform member the CCDM loss (0.0146 bit at M = 4, n = 1024); that is a known limitation of the committed v1.x PAM4 result, left in place because its output is pinned byte for byte. A candidate whose quantisation gives a positive probability a zero count raises in the matcher and is logged `infeasible` and never selected.
- Quadrature: unequal sigma always uses `quadrature="common_grid"` (also at M = 4, where a fitted channel has spread about 1.005 and the legacy path would use Gauss-Hermite, about 1e-8 bit away); exactly equal sigma uses "auto". `select_distribution` is unchanged and does not pass `quadrature`.
- Check against the existing exhaustive optimum: at M = 4, equal-sigma AWGN, PSNR 13.85 dB the continuous optimum P(+-3) is 0.36732 (SMD) and 0.36917 (BMD) against the `optimal_outer_probability` grid values 0.3675 and 0.3700 (step 0.0025); at 16 dB 0.31895 and 0.31830 against 0.3200 and 0.3175.
- Mean optical power, peak and OMA are derived from `link.mzm_intensity`; see the `optimization/constraints.py` docstring. At quadrature T(v) = L (1 + V sin(pi v / V_pi)) / 2 with L = 10^(-IL/10) and V the visibility, so T(+v) - T(-v) = L V sin(pi v / V_pi), and for any symmetric PMF the mean launched power is L P_laser / 2 (checked to 1e-12 at M = 4, 8, 16, with and without arcsine pre-distortion). With the outer levels unused, the drive swing that restores the OMA is the ratio of the amplitudes, (M - 1) / (M - 3) (3 at M = 4, 7/5 at M = 8; tested against the bracketed root). For a declared target the swing is the smallest root on the first rising branch of OMA against swing (OMA = P L V sin(pi a v / V_pi) rises to a first maximum and folds over); a target below the current OMA reduces the swing (uniform PAM4, half the OMA: 0.9202 V, not the fold-over 7.0798 V), and a target above the largest OMA reachable up to 2 V_pi is INFEASIBLE.
- Realised user rate = f_s / sps x (payload / n) k / (head + payload). Equalising by sample rate at fixed sps gives f_s' = f_s x target / rate; allowed 200 to 256 GSa/s about the 224 GSa/s nominal (-10.7 %, +14.3 %). Uniform PAM4 target with n = 1024, head 1024, payload 8192: The target is the direct uniform (2 bits per symbol, 2048 bits per block): PS70 (1912 bits per block) needs +7.1 % (inside the range), PS80 (1747) +17.2 % (above +14.3 %, INFEASIBLE). Against a CCDM-matched uniform of 2033 bits per block these were +6.3 % and +16.4 %, the wrong baseline.
- Acquisition guard: `AcquisitionId(source, id, split, content_sha256)`; `assert_independent` and `select_on_acquisitions` recompute the SHA-256 of each `Acquisition`'s samples and raise when it differs from the declared `content_sha256` (a renamed test capture with a made-up hash is refused). Bare `AcquisitionId`s (the held-out argument) are trusted as declared. `assert_independent` rejects wrong split labels, an acquisition listed twice (by id or by content under two ids), id overlap between training and test (by id alone, as in the `uxr_ingest` registry), and content-hash overlap between training and test under distinct ids. A truncated, re-exported or re-acquired copy has different bytes and is not detected; that is a documented limit, not a guarantee that no test-derived samples enter selection. `best_fixed` takes training `Acquisition`s (split and digest checked) and builds `selected_on` from them; it no longer accepts a bare channel. `assert_disjoint` remains the id-only legacy check (the PLAN row that says it checks content is wrong).
- Seed streams (generic path only): `SeedSequence([seed, 1])` simulated payload, `[seed, 2]` simulated noise (`spawn`, one child per acquisition), `[seed, 3]` Dirichlet starts. The legacy path keeps `default_rng(seed)` and demo.py's `[seed, 77]`. Simulated acquisitions are noise realisations of one shared payload; intervals over them are noise-only.

## M-PAM frame, export and profiles (v1.3-mpam C5)

Derived independently from the definitions and the plan; no other simulator was consulted.

- Alphabet: level of symbol index i is 2 i - (M - 1) (spacing 2); the Gray label of i is i XOR (i >> 1) (adjacent levels differ in one bit). `ModulationSpec(order)` is a frozen dataclass over these maps; the module functions take `order` (default 4).
- Head: preamble 512 symbols on indices 0 and M - 1 (the 511-chip m-sequence x^9 + x^5 + 1 plus one balance symbol, 256 of each), then a randomly permuted but exactly balanced training block: 512 at M = 2, 4 (the v1.2 head), 1536 at M = 8 (192 per level), 2560 at M = 16 (160 per level). Head 1024, 1024, 2048, 3072 symbols, all multiples of 1024. The training permutation uses `default_rng([seed, 0x7A11])` for every order.
- Training length: for Gaussian samples the relative standard deviation of the sample sigma is 1 / sqrt(2 (N - 1)): 5.61 % at N = 160, 5.12 % at 192, 6.27 % at 128, 12.7 % at 32. The Monte Carlo with the repository estimator gave 5.64 %, 5.09 %, 6.26 % and 12.6 %. It is a one-sigma figure.
- Direct uniform: log2 M information bits per symbol mapped straight onto Gray labels; the bit stream is `default_rng([seed, crc32(name)])` drawing `integers(0, 2, m * payload)` (m = 2 at M = 4, which is the v1.2 stream byte for byte). Shaped frames use CCDM blocks: PAM4 keeps `from_probabilities` (pinned by the anchor), M = 2, 8, 16 use `from_symmetric_probabilities`.
- Sample arithmetic: sps = f_s / baud. Protocol A: 224 GSa/s over 8 and 56 GBd gives 28 and 4. Protocol B: nominal 112 Gb/s label rate, baud = 112e9 / log2 M, sps = 224e9 / baud = 2, 4, 6, 8; M = 8 runs at 37.333 GBd. Frames hold symbols x sps samples, a multiple of 512 whenever the symbol count is a multiple of 1024, and at most 1,048,576.
- Realised-rate equalisation: rate = f_s / sps x information bits / frame symbols (`optimization/constraints.py`), target the direct uniform of the same order; needed f_s' = 224 GSa/s x (uniform bits per block / member bits per block), allowed 200 to 256 GSa/s. Reported in the metadata with `applied: false`; no waveform is re-clocked.
- DAC step diagnostic: adjacent levels are 2 / (M - 1) apart on [-1, 1]; with the mid-tread 8-bit map assumed by `dac_quantize` one unit is 127 codes, so the step is 254 / (M - 1): 254, 84.67, 36.29, 16.93 codes at M = 2, 4, 8, 16 (NRZ, full scale M - 1). An assumption: the instrument's code map is not verified.
- Pinned baseline: Uniform plus Maxwell-Boltzmann Inner and Outer at entropy deficits 0.12 and 0.28 bit below log2 M, named by centibits of entropy; declared in `configs/mpam_profiles.yaml`, loaded through `load_mpam_config`.
- `FixedOMA` (C5a): OMA against swing is P L V sin(pi V_pp a / (2 V_pi)) at quadrature, with a the largest used amplitude; its maximum, sin = 1, is at V_pp = V_pi / a (4.6154 V for the M = 16 inner levels, a = 13 / 15). The search includes swing 0 and refines the maximum, so a target equal to the maximum is reached and one above it is not.

### C5 fix round (review findings)

- Reportable baseline: `BestFixed.reportable` defaults to False and only `best_fixed` over the pinned menu sets it; the export additionally checks order, member name and PMF against the pinned menu, so a flag alone is not trusted. The v2 manifest carries `reportable`.
- Evidence: the generator writes synthetic files only and hard-codes `simulated` (as v1 does); the YAML key is removed and refused. The validator fails any evidence other than `simulated` while the metadata `source` is the synthetic string.
- Validator recomputation (written from the definitions): realised probabilities, entropy, payload bits, matcher rate (k / n of the stated composition, every block checked for that composition), matcher loss (H(target) - k / n in v2, H(realised) - k / n in v1; 0 for direct uniform), nominal rate (bits x f_s / sps), net rate (information bits / duration), protocol (A: 8 or 56 GBd; B: nominal 112 Gb/s), constraint protocol (enum), waveform peak and rms.
- Waveform scale tolerance: one 8-bit DAC code is 1/127 of full scale (mid-tread assumption). A sample more than half a code (0.5 / 127 = 3.94e-3 of full scale) from level / full_scale would be played as another code, so that is the limit; the writer's own error is below 1e-9, so the limit is loose by design and is a physical meaning, not an accuracy claim. NRZ: every sample is checked; RRC: the matched-filter output at the symbol instants.
- Preamble order: the validator generates the m-sequence x^9 + x^5 + 1 itself (Fibonacci LFSR, all-ones start, output of the last cell, 511 chips, then the balance symbol that makes 256 / 256) and compares the 512 symbols with it, so two swapped chips fail.
- Pinned menu in the validator: Maxwell-Boltzmann p_i proportional to exp(-s mu x_i^2) (s = +1 inner, -1 outer), mu by bisection to entropy log2 M - 0.12 and - 0.28 bit; agrees with `fixed_menu` to 1e-9. A waveform's PMF must be its name's PMF; `member=` of BestFixed must be one of them; Adaptive must be symmetric.
- Common-target equalisation: target = realised user rate of the PAM4 direct uniform of the same protocol, f_s / 4 x 2 x (payload blocks of 1024) / frame symbols, 110.25 Gb/s for Protocol B; needed f_s' = 224 GSa/s x target / rate(member), allowed 200 to 256 GSa/s.

## Benchmark provenance schema and fit/holdout plan (Stage 3A gate E0)

Details: `docs/benchmarks/SOURCE_REGISTER.md`.

- Implementation: `src/opticps/benchmarks/provenance.py` (frozen dataclasses, `Provenance` enum of seven values, `ErrorCode` of eight, `Checksum` with a derived MD5 state, `assert_no_split_overlap`), `catalog.py` (strict YAML loader that refuses duplicate keys, no network) and `holdout.py` (holdout guards). Rules enforced and tested: a value needs a provenance; a null value must be UNKNOWN and an UNKNOWN value must be null; a known value needs a `source_ref`; every known field is typed in the constructors (finite, positive where physical, integer modulation order in {2, 4, 8, 16}, one unit per numeric field, non-blank text, no bool, list or the string "null"); SIMULATED cannot fill a field listed in `MEASURED_FIELDS` (NOT_COMPARABLE); every asset states every field of `ASSET_FIELDS`; identifiers use character allow-lists and containers are copied into immutable ones; licence state is mandatory (LICENSE_UNVERIFIED), the status `RECORD_LEVEL` means the identifier was read in the record's raw metadata or text, unverified terms cannot grant anything, and `require_public_redistribution` also needs commercial use (a CC BY-NC-ND article fails for an MIT repository); the local MD5 and SHA-256 cannot be typed in, only `verify_local_asset` sets them, so `Md5State.VERIFIED` follows only from hashing a real file; one acquisition group, file name, supplied MD5 or local hash in two roles is LEAKAGE_DETECTED, checked by the loader; a VALIDATION or LOCKED_TEST asset is not evaluated without a committed criterion file (`require_frozen_criterion`), a BER needs a source-stated reference sequence, and only the locked test can be VALIDATED.
- Choices, not in the roadmap: INFERRED is allowed in a measured field (it is labelled, and the sample-rate unit reading "256 Gbaud sample rate" as 256 GSa/s uses it); a published setting (baud, modulation order, fibre length) is labelled PUBLISHED_MEASUREMENT because the enum has no separate "published setting" value; article and data licences are separate records; benchmark configs live in `configs/benchmarks/` and are not read by any existing module, so they cannot change an `experiment_id`.
- Fit/holdout plan: `docs/benchmarks/FIT_HOLDOUT_PLAN.md` (SHA-256 pinned in `tests/test_benchmark_provenance.py` and the TERIPHIC config). Grouping is by acquisition (one file per length), so the split is by fibre length with shared apparatus, not an independent-apparatus split. Numeric pass criteria are deliberately deferred to a hashed addendum before any 2 km statistic is computed.
- Limits: provenance labels describe what the source states, not whether it is true; read status of every external fact is `summary` (fetch tool), not `verified`. Format, calibration and reference sequence of the TERIPHIC captures are unknown and nothing here reads them.

## NANF benchmark sources, provenance vocabulary and fit/holdout plan (NANF gate N0)
