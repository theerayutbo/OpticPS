# Attribution: TERIPHIC photodiode-array dataset

The files in this directory (`STUDY_PLAN.md`, `step0_diagnostics.yaml`, `law_model.yaml`, `power_study_e2c.yaml`)
hold statistics and model outputs derived from the back-to-back and 2 km captures of the dataset named in
`docs/benchmarks/teriphic_e2/NOTICE.md` (Zenodo record 7886498, DOI 10.5281/zenodo.7886498, CC BY 4.0; creators and
funding as listed there).

Changes: the derived statistics and model outputs here were computed by OpticPS from the back-to-back and 2 km
captures only. The 10 km capture was not read. The raw data are not redistributed in this repository. Values are
summaries and model results, not samples of the captures.

Source details and the record's read status are in `docs/benchmarks/SOURCE_REGISTER.md` (Source 1).
